# Warrior of the Backwater Inn
*Keep the Peace On and Off Business Hours*

A veteran innkeeper once said, “Look here, bud. I’m just trying to run an honest business, but these damn adventuring vagrants keep showing up and wrecking the place over petty disagreements. Eventually, as a business owner, you just stop tolerating that nonsense.”

## **Level 3: Innkeeper’s Experience**
You have amassed several unique skills during your time as an innkeeper, gaining the following benefits.

***Business as Usual.*** You gain proficiency with Brewer's Supplies, Cook’s Utensils, and Carpenter’s Tools. As part of a Short Rest, you can cook a hearty meal for a number of creatures up to two plus your Dexterity modifier (minimum of three creatures) if you have ingredients and Cook’s Utensils on hand. At the end of the Short Rest, any creature who eats the food regains a number of Hit Points equal to one roll of your Martial Arts die.

***Customer Service.*** You gain proficiency in the Insight and Persuasion skills. Whenever you make an ability check that uses one of these skill proficiencies, you can roll your Martial Arts die and add the number rolled to the check.

***Emergency Measures.*** You gain proficiency with improvised weapons, which are considered Monk weapons for you.

## **Level 3: Protect the Customer**
If a creature you can see makes an attack roll against a target within 30 feet of you other than yourself, you can take a Reaction to move up to half your Speed toward that target without provoking Opportunity Attacks. If you end the movement within 5 feet of the target and within the reach or range of the attack, you can become the target of the attack roll instead. If the trigger for your Deflect Attacks feature is then met, you can use it as part of the same Reaction for this feature.

## **Level 6: Pacify the Rowdy**
When you redirect an attack’s force with your Deflect Attacks feature, it deals extra damage equal to one roll of your Martial Arts die. In addition, a creature that succeeds on its save against your Deflect Attacks feature still takes half of the damage.

You can expend the Focus Point to redirect an attack’s force with your Deflect Attacks feature even if you didn’t reduce the triggering attack’s damage to 0.

## **Level 11: Nerves of Steel**
If you fail an Intelligence, Wisdom, or Charisma saving throw, you can expend 2 Focus Points to roll your Martial Arts die and add the number rolled to the save, potentially turning failure into success. The Focus Points are expended only if the roll then succeeds. You can use this feature only once per save.

## **Level 17: Backwater Guardian**
You have two extra Reactions that can be used only for your Deflect Attacks and Protect the Customer features. You can use only one Reaction per trigger.
