<details markdown="1">
<summary markdown="span">**Augments**</summary>

An item in the Augment category, such as a rune, a magic gemstone, or a mechanical module, must be attached to a nonmagical object to use its properties.

***Attachment.*** Attaching an augment to a nonmagical object requires you to make an augmentation using Smith's Tools, Tinker's Tools, or Thieves' Tools with which you are proficient. The augmentation takes 1 hour of uninterrupted work and can be done during a Short Rest, throughout which the object and augment must be within your reach. At the end of the augmentation, you attach the augment to the object. If the augmentation is interrupted, the augment is not attached. You can detach an augment from an object by performing another augmentation on it.

***Augmented Objects.*** When at least one augment is attached to an object, that object becomes an augmented object. Augmented objects have the following rules:

**Magic Item.** An augmented object is a magic item that requires Attunement; augments can still be attached to an augmented object, ignoring this rule. You can attune to an augmented object during the same Short Rest used to attach an augment to it. Your Attunement with an augmented object immediately ends if its last augment has been removed, causing it to revert to a nonmagical object.

**No More Than Three Augments.** No more than three augments can be attached to an augmented object at a time. Any attempt to attach a fourth augment fails; you must detach an augment first. Additionally, you can’t benefit from more than one copy of an augment attached to an augmented object you are attuned to at a time.

</details>

<details markdown="1">
<summary markdown="span">**Implants**</summary>

An item in the Implant category, such as a mechanical limb, a cosmetic alteration, or an artificial organ, must be attached to a creature to use its properties.

***Body Replacements.*** Some implants replace a body part. To remove the necessary body part and attach such an implant, you can have the surgery performed on you by a creature that has proficiency in the Medicine skill and access to [[Surgeon’s Tools]] with which it is proficient. The surgery takes 2 hours of uninterrupted work and can be done during a Long Rest. The surgery doesn’t deal damage to you, and it doesn’t interrupt your Long Rest. At the end of the surgery, you gain 1 Exhaustion level (applied after the Long Rest reduces your Exhaustion level) and the implant is attached to you—the relevant body part is removed from you as part of attaching the implant. If the surgery is interrupted, you gain the Exhaustion level only.

</details>
