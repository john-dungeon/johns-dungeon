<summary markdown="span">**Implants**</summary>

An item in the Implant category might be a mechanical limb, a cosmetic alteration, or even an artificial organ that grants new abilities.

***Body Replacements.*** Some implants replace a body part. To remove the necessary body part and attach such an implant, you can have the surgery performed on you by a creature that has proficiency in the Medicine skill and access to [[Surgeon’s Tools]] with which it is proficient. The surgery takes 2 hours and can be done during a Long Rest. The surgery doesn’t deal damage to you, and it doesn’t interrupt your Long Rest. At the end of the surgery, you gain 1 Exhaustion level (after the Long Rest reduces your Exhaustion level) and the implant is attached to you—the relevant body part is removed from you as part of attaching the implant.
