casting_time: Action

concentration_max: 1 minute

description: You create a Large hand of frigid magical energy in an unoccupied space that you can see within range. The hand lasts for the duration, and it moves at your command, mimicking the movements of your hand.

The hand is an object that has AC 13 and Hit Points equal to your Hit Points maximum. If it drops to 0 Hit Points, the spell ends. The hand occupies its space and grants creatures behind it Three-Quarters Cover.

A creature killed by the hand becomes a frozen statue until it thaws.

When you cast the spell and as a Bonus Action on your later turns, you can move the hand up to 60 feet and then cause one of the following effects:

**Binding Hand.** The hand attempts to grapple a Huge or smaller creature within 5 feet of it. The target must succeed on a Dexterity saving throw, or the target has the Grappled condition, with an escape DC equal to your spell save DC. While the hand grapples the target, you can take a Bonus Action to cause the hand to freeze it, dealing 3d8 Cold damage to the target and forcing it to succeed on a Constitution saving throw or have the Restrained condition until the spell ends.

A Restrained target or a creature that can reach it can take an action to make a Strength (Athletics) check against your spell save DC. On a success, the target is no longer Restrained. Also, if a Restrained target takes Fire damage, it is no longer Restrained.

**Frozen Fist.** The hand strikes a target within 5 feet of it. Make a melee spell attack. On a hit, the target takes 4d8 Cold damage and its Speed is halved until the start of your next turn.

**Shattering Hand.** The hand explodes into shards of ice, reducing itself to 0 Hit Points. Each creature in a 20-foot-radius Sphere centered on the hand must make a Dexterity saving throw. On a failed save, a target takes 6d8 Cold damage, or 3d8 Cold damage if the hand had half its Hit Points or fewer remaining when it exploded. On a successful save, a target takes half as much damage.

***Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot.*** The damage increases by 2d8 for each spell slot level above 5.
