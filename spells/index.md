<summary markdown="span">**Esoteric Spells**</summary>

Many of the spells in this list are Esoteric spells. An Esoteric spell can’t be prepared unless a character has a feature, item, or some other effect that specifically allows them to prepare that spell.

> *For example, you might use a Tutorial Scroll, which can allow you to prepare an Esoteric spell with your Spellcasting or Pact Magic feature. Alternatively, you might use a Tome of Erudition, which allows you to memorize an Esoteric spell, making it always prepared for you.*
