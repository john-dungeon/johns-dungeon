<summary markdown="span">**Esoteric Spells**</summary>

Many of the spells in this list are Esoteric spells. An Esoteric spell can’t be prepared unless a character has a feature, item, or some other effect that allows them to prepare that spell specifically; even though these spells are part of the spell lists of certain classes, the Spellcasting or Pact Magic feature of those classes doesn't give characters the ability to prepare Esoteric spells.

> *For example, you might use a [[items:Tutorial Scroll]], which can allow you to temporarily prepare an Esoteric spell. Alternatively, you might use a [[items:Tome of Erudition]], which allows you to memorize an Esoteric spell, making it always prepared for you.*
