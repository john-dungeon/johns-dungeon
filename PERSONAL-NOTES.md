# Great Luminant Lore

- Many species in the galaxy called the Great Luminant resemble each other because the Progenitors, the first explorers of the galaxy and who were once among the only life in the universe, sowed seeds of life from their own planet all across the galaxy. Life forms that ones that originated independently from the Progenitors are called aberrant species.
- There are two main modes of cosmic travel: superluminal and warp. Superluminal travel is mostly facilitated by an ancient Progenitor technology called the Tachyon engine. Tachyon engines and superluminal travel are only used to cross voids, since ramming into matter at hyperspeed would obliterate any spacecraft. Even then, superluminal spacecraft have to deploy "slip shields" to prevent direct collision with stray matter. Warp travel is facilitated by a different, more recent technology called the TPM system, which creates a wormhole connecting two specified coordinates, which are relative to the planet Meridian Prime, where the technology was invented. Intuitively, this technology is more recent than the Tachyon engine because it only links known coordinates, whereas the Tachyon engine could be used by the Progenitors to explore unknown frontiers and sow the seeds of life throughout the Great Luminant. The TPM system was created by imitating the ability of Girtablullu, one of several eldritch monstrosities that live in the Great Luminant. Girtablullu is a massive pincered creature that digests celestial bodies with its "tail", which essentially injects digestive matter into a body. It typically eats asteroids and other relatively small celestial bodies, but has ventured to eat dwarf planets before. It uses its "pincers" to rip wormholes in spacetime, which it uses to leap large distances across the Great Luminant.
- The TPM system's full name is the TEPO-PERI-MITRA integrated system, and each of those names is its own acronym for one of the system's three subunits. The Trajectory Ephemeris Propagation Operator (TEPO) calculates the positions of known locations relative to Meridian Prime, using a galactic database of celestial bodies and their velocities. The Paired Exotic Rift Initiator (PERI) opens a non-orientable wormhole and layers exotic matter into the throat of the wormhole to keep it open. However, due to the nature of the wormhole, anything that enters would exit as its perfect mirror image, causing slow and catastrophic damage to life forms. The Möbius Inversion Transit Rectifier Array (MITRA) layers a second orientation-reversing twist in the throat of the wormhole, reversing the mirror-flipping and allowing things that enter to exit safely with the same molecular handedness.
- Girtablullu is an aberrant species whose body has complete achirality. Unlike the TPM system, which has a mechanism to reverse the inversion of molecular chirality caused by crossing through non-orientable wormholes, the Girtablullu's spacetime rifts have one inversion. However, Girtablullu suffers no effects from its molecules being inverted because its entire body is achiral, meaning the molecules are identical to their mirror images.
