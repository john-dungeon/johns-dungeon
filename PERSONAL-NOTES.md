# great luminant

- many species in the great luminant resemble each other because the Progenitors, the first explorers of the galaxy and what was once among the only life in the universe, sowed seeds of life from their own planet all across the galaxy. life that doesn't resemble the progenitors at all, ones that originated independently from them, are called aberrant species.
