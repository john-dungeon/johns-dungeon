# Warrior of the Storm
*Rush with the Might of a Storm*

Warriors of the Storm strike and maneuver with blinding speed, harnessing the chaotic power of thunder, wind, and lightning. By emulating the natural splendor of storms, they become unstoppable forces of nature themselves.

## Eccentric Nature
Warriors of the Storm often have strange quirks relating to their affinity with nature.

At your option, roll on or choose a result from the Quirks of the Storm table to determine a unique quirk for yourself.

| Quirks of the Storm |  |
| --- | --- |
| **d6** | **Quirk** |
| 1 | Your natural voice is loud and thunderous. |
| 2 | Your body tingles with static electricity, causing your hair to stand up. |
| 3 | Your eyes emit a small amount of flickering light. |
| 4 | Your hair always moves as if blown by the wind, even if there is no wind. |
| 5 | Small clouds hover around your head and indicate your mood. |
| 6 | Wind from an unknown source blows dramatically whenever you enter a room. |

## **Level 3: Stormfall Arts**
You have learned to wield thunder and lightning, gaining the following benefits.

***Fulgurant Rush.*** You can expend 1 Focus Point to cast the *Misty Step* spell. When you cast that spell, you can teleport up to your Speed, instead of up to 30 feet, and a thunderous boom is audible within 300 feet of the space you leave.

***Harnessed Tempest.*** You know the *Shocking Grasp* and *Thunderclap* cantrips. Wisdom is your spellcasting ability for them. When you use your Flurry of Blows, you can replace one of the Unarmed Strikes with a casting of one of these cantrips.

## **Level 6: Fulminant March**
You can expend 2 Focus Points to cast the *Misty Step* spell as a Reaction in response to taking damage.
In addition, whenever you cast that spell, you can choose one of the following additional effects.

***Bolt.*** Creatures within 5 feet of the space you left or the space you appear in (your choice) take damage equal to one roll of your Martial Arts die. The damage type is your choice of Lightning or Thunder.

***Squall.*** Immediately before you teleport, you can touch a willing creature or choose a creature Grappled by you. When you teleport, the creature teleports to an unoccupied space of your choice within 5 feet of your destination space. If the creature was Grappled by you, the teleport doesn’t end your grapple with it.

## **Level 11: Tempest Soul**
You embody the storm, gaining the following benefits.

***Damage Resistance.*** You gain Resistance to Lightning and Thunder damage.

***Fulminant Flurry.*** When you use Flurry of Blows, you can replace up to two of the Unarmed Strikes, each with a casting of the *Shocking Grasp* or *Thunderclap* cantrip.

## **Level 17: Eye of the Storm**
As a Magic action, you can expend 5 Focus Points to invoke a storm of black clouds that surrounds you in a 20-foot Emanation for 1 minute, until you have the Incapacitated condition, or until you dismiss it (no action required). That area is Lightly Obscured, and you and your allies have Half Cover while in the area.

Whenever the Emanation enters a creature's space and whenever a creature enters the Emanation or ends its turn there, you can force that creature to make a Constitution saving throw. On a failed save, the target takes Lightning or Thunder damage (your choice) equal to three rolls of your Martial Arts die and has the Prone condition. On a successful save, the target takes half as much damage only. A creature makes this save only once per turn.

When you take the Attack action on your turn while this feature is active, you can replace one of your attacks with a staggering bolt of lightning. Make a ranged spell attack against one creature within 60 feet using Wisdom as your spellcasting ability. On a hit, the target takes Lightning damage equal to two rolls of your Martial Arts die and must succeed on a Constitution saving throw or have the Incapacitated condition until the start of your next turn.
