name: Dwarf
description: Dwarves are the denizens of the earth, born and raised in the deep, dark underground. They are perfectly adapted to subterranean life, with superb night vision and the ability to sense things by the vibrations they make in the ground. Due to the wealth of resources found underground, dwarves have also developed a robust artisan culture and are known all across Erisia for their craftsmanship.

Dwarves are short and stout, built for navigating small subterranean crevices. Among men and women alike, dwarves are muscular and often sport robust beards. They use their small size and great strength to bore tunnels through the earth by hand, mining it for its precious minerals and magical materials.

### Trivia: Eternal Grudge

Dwarves and elves share a mutual grudge that spans millennia, lasting so long because of both species’ long lifespans. Though its origins have long been forgotten, few find the feud surprising. Dwarves champion mastery over stone and steel, determined to shape the world in their image, while elves revere the untouched beauty of the natural world, even worshiping it in folk religions. Such opposing ideals have fueled conflict for generations, whatever first ignited it. Several attempts have been made to unite the two species in harmony, but no truce has ever lasted longer than a few years.

name: Troll
description: Trolls are tribal folk who possess robust physiques and extreme regenerative abilities. Being more resilient than almost any other species, trolls have established homes in nearly every biome in Erisia, except for extremely hot environments like the Sea of Fire. In the past, hartens and trolls had a strained relationship, as hartens would indiscriminately attack trolls, mistaking them for monsters due to their immense stature, imposing appearances, and monstrous regeneration. However, since trolls are resilient and relatively patient, tensions eventually smoothed over. Nowadays, trolls are generally known for their work as wandering shamans in harten societies, using bizarre blood rites to tell and shift fortunes.

Trolls are tall individuals, typically with lean builds rather than muscular ones. They typically have green skin tones, but also other cool colors such as blue or purple. At a glance, they might resemble orcs. However, their most distinguishing features are their giant tusks, which curve far out of their lower jaws like an elephant’s tusks. To trolls, the longer one’s tusks, the more noble the individual. Special care is given to these tusks, which are often also adorned with jewelry and painted designs.

### Trivia: Yaksin Haji

Due to their regenerative abilities, trolls rarely die from external causes. Instead, their bodies have an invisible internal timer. Once a troll approaches the end of this timer, their regenerative abilities begin to fail them. Wounds begin to close in strange ways, forming unusual and often extremely painful growths, and large-scale regeneration may even result in the budding of new limbs. As a result, unlike their younger selves, older trolls often work hard to preserve their bodies, despite being just as strong as they were in their primes. However, injuries are inevitable, and an old troll will eventually suffer crippling pain as its body fails. Thus, troll culture has developed a funerary rite of fire, called Yaksin Haji, in which an individual is given an anaesthetic extract from the frostroot flower and burned on an altar to grant them a peaceful death.
