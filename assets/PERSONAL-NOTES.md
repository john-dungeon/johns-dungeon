# Great Luminant Lore

- Many species in the galaxy called the Great Luminant resemble each other because the Progenitors, the first explorers of the galaxy and who were once among the only life in the universe, sowed seeds of life from their own planet all across the galaxy. Life forms that ones that originated independently from the Progenitors are called aberrant species.
- There are two main modes of cosmic travel: superluminal and warp. Superluminal travel is mostly facilitated by an ancient Progenitor technology called the Tachyon engine, which is named after the research organization that created it but has nothing to do with the superluminal particle that is also its namesake. Tachyon engines and superluminal travel are only used to cross voids, since ramming into matter at hyperspeed would obliterate any spacecraft. Even then, superluminal spacecraft have to deploy "slip shields" to prevent direct collision with stray matter. Warp travel is facilitated by a different, more recent technology called the TPM system, which creates a wormhole connecting two specified coordinates, which are relative to the planet Meridian Prime, where the technology was invented. Intuitively, this technology is more recent than the Tachyon engine because it only links known coordinates, whereas the Tachyon engine could be used by the Progenitors to explore unknown frontiers and sow the seeds of life throughout the Great Luminant. The TPM system was created by imitating the ability of Girtablullu, one of several eldritch monstrosities that live in the Great Luminant. Girtablullu is a massive cosmic creature that digests celestial bodies with its "tail", which essentially injects digestive matter into a body. It typically eats asteroids and other relatively small celestial bodies, but has ventured to eat dwarf planets before. It uses its "pincers" to rip wormholes in spacetime, which it uses to leap large distances across the Great Luminant.
- The TPM system's full name is the TEPO-PERI-MITRA integrated system, and each of those names is its own acronym for one of the system's three subunits. The Trajectory Ephemeris Propagation Operator (TEPO) calculates the positions of known locations relative to Meridian Prime, using a galactic database of celestial bodies and their velocities. The Paired Exotic Rift Initiator (PERI) opens a non-orientable wormhole and layers exotic matter into the throat of the wormhole to keep it open. However, due to the nature of the wormhole, anything that enters would exit as its perfect mirror image, causing slow and catastrophic damage to life forms. The Möbius Inversion Transit Rectifier Array (MITRA) layers a second orientation-reversing twist in the throat of the wormhole, reversing the mirror-flipping and allowing things that enter to exit safely with the same molecular handedness.
- Girtablullu is an aberrant species whose body is perfectly bisymmetrical and whose molecules have complete achirality. Unlike the TPM system, which has a mechanism to reverse the inversion of molecular chirality caused by crossing through non-orientable wormholes, the Girtablullu's spacetime rifts have one inversion. However, Girtablullu suffers no effects from its molecules being inverted because all its molecules are achiral, meaning they are identical to their mirror images and virtually unchanged when they cross a non-orientable wormhole.

# Morwald Lore

- After the demon lord, a legendary necromancer lich named Maliketh T‘sal Karmis was killed, the magic within his body dissipated, releasing all the undead he had controlled. Chaos ensued, as some undead gained freedom and others lost all semblance of self-control, and the people, being too weak after waging war with the demon lord for decades, were not prepared to defeat the uncontrolled undead.
- Factions:
  - **The Hivemind**: The Hivemind is a single megacolony of fungal-type undead with multiple central nodes, or “kings”. The existence of multiple of these kings allows the colony to persist as one superorganism even if one hivemind center dies. It’s as if a human being had multiple brains, and so it could afford brain death in one and still live.
The Liberated: Based in the city of Necropolis, this faction of sapient-type undead is one of the primary undead factions. They seek out humanoid flesh as a necessity, but don’t necessarily enjoy the killing of people.
The Sanguine: The Sanguine are a secret cabal of vampires, and they have politically taken over a city from the shadows, silently driving out all the holy magic users and forcing the remainder to bow to them and act as their livestock.
  - **The Scavengers**: The Scavengers pluck fallen civilizations clean and use custom weaponized carriages drawn by golems, mutant animals, or even zombies to mow through hordes of undead.
  - **The Subterraneans**: The Subterraneans are primarily made of dwarves and live in underground tunnels to avoid the undead.
  - **The Lost**: One unique faction, called the Lost, has somehow found a way to completely mask their presence to certain types of undead, allowing them to wander amongst the living dead as if they were invisible.
  - **The Hallowed**: The Hallowed are a faction of holy magic users and their followers, who have developed techniques to repel undeath curses and physically fight against the other types of undead.
  - **The Ironclad**: The Ironclad, based in the megacity of Turan, live within a city surrounded by multiple layers of walls, and have one of the world’s most powerful militaries. However, this particular faction is also besieged by the worst of the apocalypse, being victim to all types of undead at once. Over the past decade, 2 of their walls have been breached, losing them 2 sectors of their megacity.
  - **The Scions**: The Scions are dhampirs, the offspring of vampires and humanoids. Being of both worlds but of none at all, they are shunned by both types of societies and have gathered to form their own. Dhampirs are possible because undeath is a spectrum, and vampires are a special class of curse-type undead that are more living than they are dead. Therefore, they can sometimes reproduce with humanoids.
  - **The Hunters**: The Hunters are a small faction of undead slayers, derived from an ancient family of vampire hunters. They possess a vast wealth of anti-undead knowledge, primarily using talismans, ancient artifacts, strange practical techniques like using hallowed salt or silver weapons, and specialized weaponry for specific types of undead. Essentially, they are like the Belmonts in Castlevania.
  - **The Choir**: The Choir is a faction of undead-worshiping lunatics. They infiltrate and raid humanoid communities to take sacrifices for the undead overlord, whom they call the Avatar of Maliketh. In exchange, the Avatar protects them from other undead through mysterious means. Not even the Choir knows what the Avatar truly is, but as long as they keep feeding it, they remain safe.
  - **The Symbionts**: The Symbionts are a faction of parasite-type undead. However, their strain of parasites is unique, working in tandem with them and granting them superhuman abilities that allow them to efficiently hunt humanoids of their own volition. These types of parasites also have mind-altering abilities on their hosts, and are able to transform an unwilling victim into a willing perpetrator over time.
  - **The Legion**: The Legion is a small army of echo knights. Their kingdom has disappeared already, but they loyally march on as a unit in hopes of finding their fallen kingdom, slaying whatever undead may be in their path. Due to their strong wills and immortal nature, they come back to life as soon as they are killed, collectively unable to acknowledge that they died even after being torn apart on the battlefield; they are a nearly unstoppable independent faction. These echoes are unique in that their shared conviction strengthens one another’s wills, so they don’t naturally deteriorate as other echoes do. Should they ever be made aware that they are marching toward an impossible goal, however, they would surely perish quickly.
  - **The Shepherds**: The Shepherds are a small community of necromancers who have arisen in the wake of civilization’s fall. Previously, all necromancers, whether aligned with Maliketh or not, were persecuted or killed, but in a world of undead, they thrive. They can control multiple types of undead, but not all, so they seek to find Maliketh’s research to fill their missing knowledge. These necromancers are amiable and don’t seek out violence, but their morality is too “evil” to consider them a “good faction”, making them “neutral” overall.
  - **The Surgeons**: The Surgeons are a small community of lunatic scientists who have learned to harness the abilities of chimera-type undead for themselves. They graft foreign body parts to themselves, absorbing them to wield great power to fight back against the apocalypse. In other words, this faction is technically a “good guy” faction that fights fire with fire.
  - **The Transcendents**: The Transcendents are a tiny faction of Maliketh-aligned intelligent undead that intentionally bear multiple types of undeath. Each of its 8 members has supernatural authority over one of the 8 types of undead. Their combination of biological supremacy and magical enhancement had not been theorized by Maliketh, but was created by the Transcendents after the apocalypse began, and has proven extremely effective. This combination has essentially created immortal undead demigods. They intend to resurrect Maliketh because they believe he can restore order to this apocalyptic world, and intend to do so after wiping out all traces of goddess worship, since that is what ultimately killed Maliketh. Unfortunately, due to their cursed nature, they are also vulnerable to holy magic, though far less than normal cursed-type undead, which means the crusade against the goddess has been difficult so far. The Transcendents could technically end the apocalypse together, but none of them trust each other, and would rather be equal under a greater power.
  - Multiple factions of undead have formed in this dying world. Informal factions of undead have also begun fighting amongst themselves, tearing each other apart in perpetual war amongst rotting immortals.
  - Living humanoid societies are typically surrounded by massive impenetrable walls, protected by the magic of the goddess, have some sort of means to hide from undead, or are simply too strong for their local undead to take over.
- Holy magic can be used against other magical phenomena and is especially effective against dark magic, which means it can dispel and destroy curse-type undead. However, it offers little to counter biological undead, like bacteria and fungal-types.
- The demon lord made multiple unique types of undead since the church of the goddess created a perfect counter against his original necromancy:
  - **Curse-type undead**: the original type and perfectly countered by holy magic, being effectively dispelled and/or destroyed by holy magic. It includes magical reanimation, vampires, and mummies. Curse-type undead spread when things die in dark magic zones or when they are killed by curse-type undead. Functionally, curse-type undead are just like other infectious undead diseases, like bacteria or viruses, but transmit through a self-replicating spell/curse that corrupts one’s soul and distorts their body.
  - **Fungus-type undead**: it works as a hivemind, sharing all the information a single branch gathers with the entire colony through underground hyphae. Colonies are centered around a “patient zero” host, which acts as a central command node. If the host is incapacitated, the entire colony is incapacitated. If a host dies, the colony is incapacitated until a new host takes control. Often, this just results in the death of the colony since an incapacitated colony lacks the direction to create a new host, and can only be absorbed by other active colonies that have hosts. Fungus-type undead spread through physical contact, specifically when spores root themselves on a person. Fungus-type undead impact their environments the most, settling down and uncontrollably reshaping places into colonial centers full of hyphae and tentacles.
  - **Chimera-type undead**: special monstrous undead that eat living beings and assimilate them into their own bodies, forming a chimera that possesses all the abilities of their components. Chimera-type undead are most dangerous when they devour each other, compounding all their accumulated abilities. Beyond a certain point, however, chimera biology can no longer support their absorbed functions and their bodies begin to break down. From their corpses, new chimeras are born, inheriting fragments of the parent’s abilities.
  - **Sapient-type undead**: sapient-type undead are not infected dead but a species of their own, with traditional reproduction methods and intelligent societies. They feed on humanoid flesh as a primary food source and possess superhuman abilities, including regeneration and resurrection. Their grotesque corpse-like appearances are due to the unethical biological engineering of the demon lord to produce the perfect intelligent immortal killing machines.
  - **Bacteria-type undead**: Among the most dangerous type of undead, bacteria-types spread through airborne bacteria. Contaminant zones can turn anyone into zombies when they breathe the air. Certain bacteria-type undead exist only as suicide bombers, spreading the zombie disease as an aura and releasing concentrated clouds of bacteria upon death. Upon infection, the bacteria eat a body from the inside out to reproduce while simultaneously triggering the corpses to wander near other humanoids, turning them into walking vectors of infection.
  - **Virus-type undead**: Viral-type undead spread via bites. Unlike most other undead, they are characterized by the slow deterioration of people’s minds, slowly turning them into flesh-hungry monsters that either eat themselves or eat others and spread the virus. As a result, they don’t last very long, relatively speaking, and never come back to life like the other undead types. Virus-type undead are still considered undead because their nature is akin to possession, like that of ghosts. The victims are prisoners in their own body, being fully conscious as their bodies deteriorate and rot away, being unable to control violent urges. Virus-type undead are also extremely prone to mutations that turn them into superpowered monstrosities. Like bacteria-types, virus-types can infect animals, but they can take advantage of animal biology to spread far better than bacteria-types.
  - **Memory-type undead**: the demon lord’s greatest feat of power was destroying the barrier between life and the afterlife. Nowadays, when people die with sufficient regret, willpower, or any other powerful denial of their own mortality, they may rise again as an “echo”. Echos are undoubtedly undead, frozen in time and unable to age, and no longer needing to eat, drink, sleep, or otherwise, preserved perfectly from their moment of death. However, they and others around them may not even be aware that they are dead, since they perfectly resemble themselves in life. There will be signs, however, of the deterioration of their soul, as they begin flickering in and out of existence and forgetting themselves when their will wavers. As their memory breaks down further, an echo may begin to devolve into a simple, unintelligent creature that acts indiscriminately upon the individual’s strongest desire. Unfortunately, this desire is more often than not violent. When an echo’s desires are fulfilled, they fade away, passing on to the afterlife as they should have.
  - **Parasite-type undead**: perhaps the most insidious type of undead, the parasite is a worm that infects a host’s brain and pilots it while it is unconscious to kill and consume other humanoids. However, unless a murder can be solved, no one will ever know that a parasite is the cause. Parasites can also wrest control from their hosts when a host’s actions may result in the death of the parasite. When a parasite reaches maturity, it will burrow into a host’s nasal cavity and lay its eggs, then force the host to drown itself in a nearby body of water. Any body of water will do, but the parasites are specifically looking for rivers, where other humanoids will drink from. In well-drinking civilizations, parasites have been observed piloting hosts to dive into wells. This has, in turn, resulted in those civilizations draconically guarding their water supplies.

# Orobis

- A dark fog wall is actively closing in around the world, devouring everything in its path. Welterbeasts spawn out of the fog to destroy all things.
- People have calculated that the fog circle is centered at a certain place they call Heaven. They believe that if they can reach Heaven, they will be saved from impending doom.
