<summary markdown="span">**Charged Actions**</summary>

A Charged Action is an action that a monster can take at the end of its turn, but it doesn’t take effect until the start of the monster’s next turn. The monster can’t take a Charged Action if it has the Incapacitated condition or is otherwise unable to take actions.

When a monster takes a Charged Action, it chooses its targets or specifies the area of effect of its action, but the action doesn't take effect until the start of the monster’s next turn. If the monster has multiple turns, the action takes effect at the start of the monster’s next turn on the same Initiative that the action was initially taken. Charged Actions often have conditions that targets must meet in order to be targeted. For example, a **Young White Dragon** might have a Charged Action called Permafrost Breath that targets each creature in a 60-foot Cone. If a creature moves out of the area before the action takes effect, it no longer fulfills the conditions of the action and is no longer targeted by it.

Before a Charged Action takes effect, it can be interrupted in the following ways, ending it with no effect:

**Inactive or Dead.** The monster dies or an effect causes it to be unable to take actions, such as the Incapacitated condition.

**Special Conditions.** Some Charged Actions have special ways to interrupt them, such as destroying a certain object or hitting the monster a certain number of times, as noted in its description.

The monster can also choose to interrupt its own Charged Action (no action required).
