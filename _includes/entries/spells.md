name: Ghost Bolt
level: Cantrip
school: Necromancy
classes: Cleric, Warlock, Wizard
casting_time: Action
ritual: false
range: 30 feet
components: V, S
duration: Instantaneous
tags: Esoteric, Saving Throw
description: Describe or name a creature that has dealt damage to you within 1 hour of the spell’s casting and send a bolt of vengeful energy after it. If the creature is within range, it must succeed on a Dexterity saving throw or take 1d8 Necrotic damage. The bolt phases in and out of the Ethereal Plane until it reaches the target; the target gains no benefit from Half Cover, Three-Quarters Cover, or Total Cover for this save.

***Cantrip Upgrade.*** The damage increases by 1d8 when you reach levels 5 (2d8), 11 (3d8), and 17 (4d8).
