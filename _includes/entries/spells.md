name: Ghost Bolt
level: Cantrip
school: Necromancy
classes: Cleric, Warlock, Wizard
casting_time: Action
ritual: false
range: 30 feet
components: V, S
duration: Instantaneous
tags: Esoteric
description: Describe or name a creature that you’ve seen within 1 hour of the spell’s casting. You summon a bolt of spiritual energy that seeks out that creature; the spell fails if the target isn’t within range. Make a ranged spell attack against the target. If you can’t see the target, this attack roll doesn’t have Disadvantage as a result of that (though it might still have Disadvantage for other reasons). On a hit, the target takes 1d8 Necrotic damage.

The bolt phases in and out of the Ethereal Plane until it reaches the target—the target gains no benefit from Half Cover or Three-Quarters Cover against this spell, and if the target is behind Total Cover, you can still target it with the spell.

***Cantrip Upgrade.*** The damage increases by 1d8 when you reach levels 5 (2d8), 11 (3d8), and 17 (4d8).
