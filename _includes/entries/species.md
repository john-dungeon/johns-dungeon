name: Troll
description: Trolls are tribal folk who possess robust physiques and extreme regenerative abilities. Being more resilient than almost any other species, trolls have established homes in nearly every biome in Erisia, except for the extremely hot environments like the Sea of Fire. In the past, humans and trolls had a strained relationship, as humans would indiscriminately attack trolls, mistaking them for monsters due to their immense stature, imposing appearances, and superhuman abilities. However, since trolls are resilient and relatively patient, tensions eventually smoothed over. Nowadays, trolls are generally known for their work as wandering shamans in human societies, using strange rites of blood to tell and shift fortunes.

Trolls are tall individuals, typically with lean builds rather than muscular ones. They typically have green skin tones, but also other cool colors such as blue or purple. At a glance, they might resemble orcs. However, their most distinguishing features are their giant tusks, which curve far out of their lower jaws like an elephant’s tusks. To trolls, the longer one’s tusks, the more noble the individual. Special care is given to these tusks, which are often also adorned with jewelry and painted designs.

### Trivia: Yaksin Haji

Due to their regenerative abilities, trolls rarely die from external causes. Instead, their bodies have an invisible internal timer. Once a troll approaches the end of this timer, their regenerative abilities begin to fail them. Wounds begin to close in strange ways, forming unusual and often extremely painful growths, and large-scale regeneration may even result in the budding of new limbs. As a result, unlike their younger selves, older trolls often work hard to preserve their bodies, despite being just as strong as they were in their prime. However, injuries are inevitable, and an old troll will eventually suffer crippling pain as its body fails. Thus, troll culture has developed a funerary rite of fire, called Yaksin Haji, in which an individual is given an anaesthetic extract from the frostroot flower and burned on an altar to grant them peaceful death.
